## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/profile-pic.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

My experience spans instrumentation, hardware integration, manufacturing, testing, and systems engineering through research, design projects, and multidisciplinary engineering teams. I am particularly interested in spacecraft systems and the development of scientific instruments for ground- and space-based observatories, and I am seeking opportunities to contribute to the next generation of space missions.
